# Where to look, UCB

**Where to look, UCB**: choosing where to sample next by upper confidence bound, the posterior mean plus a multiple of its standard deviation, inside a Bayesian optimization loop that fits, picks the next point, samples it and refits

**Tutorials:** the Upper Confidence Bound section of [*Exploring Bayesian Optimization*](https://distill.pub/2020/bayesian-optimization/) (Distill), and the loop in [Bayesian optimization](https://github.com/krasserm/bayesian-machine-learning/blob/dev/bayesian-optimization/bayesian_optimization.ipynb)'s "Implementation with NumPy and SciPy" section. Then steps 9–12 of the Bench worksheet.
